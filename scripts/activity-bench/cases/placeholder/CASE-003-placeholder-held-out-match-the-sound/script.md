# PLACEHOLDER: Match the sound

This is a placeholder case for building and testing the activity bench. It is not a real case.

The child hears a dog bark. The narrator asks: "Who made that sound?"
A cat, a dog and a cow are shown. Choosing the dog makes it wag its tail and the narrator says:
"Woof! It was the dog." Any other animal makes its own sound and the narrator says: "Listen
again." The activity ends after the dog is chosen.
