# Motion for learning

- **Date:** 2026-10-10
- **Type:** feat
- **Scope:** `plugins`, `server`

The scene composition skill has a new section, "Motion for learning":

- move things to show a change the narration is about, never only to decorate;
- hold each result still for at least a second;
- pair every important change with something that stays, such as a label, an arrow or the
  narration naming it;
- never flash more than three times a second (WCAG 2.3.1);
- let celebrations play once and come to rest.

The critique rubric's motion score now asks the same. The rules are adapted from open-design's
animation-discipline craft note (Apache-2.0), itself adapted from refero_skill (MIT, © Refero
Design).
