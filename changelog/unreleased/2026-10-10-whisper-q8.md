# The narration check downloads a smaller Whisper model

- **Date:** 2026-10-10
- **Type:** fix
- **Scope:** `server`

The scene video's final check transcribes narration with local Whisper. It now loads the model's
8-bit (`q8`) weights instead of its full-precision ones, so its first use downloads about 77 MB
(a 23 MB encoder and a 54 MB decoder) instead of about 291 MB. This applies to both the English
and the multilingual model. A full-precision copy already downloaded stays on disk but is no
longer read, and can be deleted from the server's `models/audio` folder.

On activity test16, scene 2's timeline narration transcribes word for word at 8 bits, as it does at full
precision: 100% word accuracy, against the check's 90%.
